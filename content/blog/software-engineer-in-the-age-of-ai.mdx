A decade ago, software engineering's greatest artifact aside from designing scalable and robust systems, was the written code that could be easily understood and maintained by others. There's a huge focus more on the quality of codebase and the ability to write code that can be easily understood and maintained by others. There's no surprise about this given that systems are not worked on by a single person but rather a group of people that will build on top of what others build. It's pretty much essential for codebases to be easily understood, extendable and maintanable - which is one of the important things that a software engineer must do.

## A shift in the industry

Going back to the present, it's pretty much evident how the industry is quickly shifting especially when it comes to software and how things are being done. With the rise of AI, we can see pretty much how workflows are being shaped and changed. Before, reading through documentation and having extreme expertise in a particular library or framework takes time. These days, you can just ask AI about anything without really going through the documentation yourself. You can ask questions and it'll give you the answer based on what it can go through the website you link it to.
